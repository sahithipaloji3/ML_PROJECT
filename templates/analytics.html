WHY RECALL MATTERS

Measure missed cases, not just correct guesses

Recall tells us how many actual attrition cases a model identifies. A low recall model may miss employees who later leave—but increasing recall can also create more false alarms.

!
FAIRNESS & LIMITATIONS

A flag is not a conclusion

False positives can unfairly label employees. Any future model needs careful validation, subgroup review, privacy safeguards, and human oversight. Correlation is not causation.
